Here is the high level flow diagram of our applicaiton. 
We have leveraged, Flask framework to act as a bridge between the frontend(html/CSS) and the backend application(python).
When we run the flask application, it kicksoff the frontend part to acquire the language option, and when we press on start from the frontend, flask initiates Signlanguage interpreter 
application.
Signlanguage interpreter has two core components Keypoint classifier and history classifier. They are responsible for model creation and training respectively. 
Once the training part is done, application can identify those gestures indefinitely.
